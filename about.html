About the College

Established in 1959, DBS College has been a beacon of learning and discipline.


Our Vision & Mission

Dayanand Brijendra Swarup P.G. College has been established by Late Dr. Virendra Swarup, with the objective to facilitate a comprehensive learning environment in which faculty, staff and students can explore, examine, preserve and disseminate the values, knowledge and wisdom which is a prerequisite to ensure the upliftment of current and future generations. The college seeks to provide the students a learning that aims to empower them in finding best of both cultural and material achievements. The college also aims to carry the torch of knowledge to the extreme edges where higher education is yet to blossom.
